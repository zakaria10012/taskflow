FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How does the AI daily plan work?

Every morning, TaskFlow AI analyzes your tasks, deadlines, calendar, and energy patterns to generate a prioritized plan. It blocks focus time, surfaces what's overdue, and auto-reschedules anything that slips — you just show up and execute.

Can I import from my current tool?

Yes. One-click import from Asana, Trello, Linear, Notion, Jira, and Todoist. Your projects, labels, and due dates come through intact, and AI re-prioritizes them on first load.

Is my data secure?

Always. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypt data at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3, and never train AI models on your private data. Enterprise plans add SSO, SCIM, and audit logs.

What happens when I cancel?

You keep full access until the end of your billing period. After that, your account reverts to the free Starter plan — your data stays, read-only, for 90 days so you can export or return anytime.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

Yes — 50% off Pro for eligible early-stage startups (under 10 employees, under $2M raised) and registered nonprofits. Reach out to our team and we'll get you set up.
